Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what Pelex collects, why, where it is kept, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. Pelex is an AI assistant and business record platform operated from Nigeria by PelexSkillHub (“Pelex”, “we”, “us”).

Questions and requests: hello@pelex.tech · Support: support@pelex.tech

3How we use information

  • To provide the features you use: chat, business records, analysis, document reading, scheduled reports, Spotlight and payments.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and apply usage limits.
  • To send service emails you asked for or that the service needs, such as sign-up confirmation, password help and receipts.
  • To keep the service reliable. We do not use your business records or chats to train public AI models.

4Where it is stored and processed

These providers handle data only to provide their part of the service:

  • Our hosting and database services: store your accounts, business records and files, and keep the service running. Access is controlled by database rules, so each business sees only its own data. Live updates reach only the business that owns the record.
  • Security checks: a human-verification check and a protective layer guard the service against misuse.
  • AI model services: the messages you send to chat, and any picture you attach to a chat, go to the AI service that answers them. Pelex removes sensitive details where it can first. That service’s own terms also apply. You can use the business tools without any AI feature.
  • Paystack and Flutterwave: payment processing. They receive your payment details directly, and send payment notices to Pelex.
  • Email delivery provider: sends service emails.

Some providers store data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on their contractual safeguards.

6Google sign-in and Google Workspace data

Google sign-in is not Gmail access. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Pelex receives your basic Google profile information (name, email address and profile picture) through our database service Auth so you can sign in. It does not give Pelex permission to read your Gmail, Calendar, Drive or Sheets data.

Current connector status

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Sheets connectors are not enabled in Pelex. The Gmail Bot currently helps you draft email text and can open a compose window in Gmail. You review and send that email yourself. Pelex does not read your Gmail inbox, messages, attachments or labels, does not send messages through the Gmail API, and does not access your Calendar, Drive or Sheets content through Google APIs.

If a Google Workspace connector is introduced later, Pelex will explain the feature and request specific permissions on Google’s consent screen before accessing that data. You can decline or disconnect it. This policy will be updated before any connector is made available, to describe the data accessed, how it is used, where it is processed, how long it is kept, and how to disconnect or request deletion.

  • Pelex does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
  • Pelex does not use Google Workspace user data to train AI models.
  • When and if a Google API connector is enabled, Pelex’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Any future email-sending connector will require your explicit approval before a message is sent.

7Sharing

We share information only with the providers listed in section 4, with people you invite to a business you own, with authorities when the law requires, and to protect people’s safety. We do not sell personal data.

8Your rights and choices

  • Access and copy: you can see your business records, chats and saved videos in the app, and ask for a copy of your data.
  • Correct: you can edit your profile and business records.
  • Delete: you can delete chats, uploads, saved videos and business records, and delete your account. Deleted uploads can be restored for one year.
  • Withdraw: you can turn off chat saving, change keep-times, and stop optional features at any time. Google sign-in authenticates your account; it does not connect a Google Workspace account.
  • Complain: you may contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To use a right, write to hello@pelex.tech. We respond within 30 days.

9Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, database rules so that each business sees only its own data, an activity record for business changes, and separate keys for each service. Keys are kept on the server and never placed in the website. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10Children

Pelex is not for children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes. For important changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
